# ADR 020: Drained food nutrition

Status: **Accepted for N8, 2026-11-17**.
Requirements: CALC-002, CALC-011, CALC-014, CALC-015, DATA-003, DATA-006.

## Australian source boundary

The primary-source check in [food-state nutrition research](../food-state-nutrition-research.md)
records the conditional drained-food basis in Standard 1.2.8-12 and the
unresolved overlap with other preparation provisions. It does not establish
universal NIP applicability or a formula for nutrient distribution during
draining. N8 adds no regulatory rule or legal threshold. Results remain
drafts requiring review, not certification.

## Decision

- Record the draining directions and direct, dated nutrition evidence for
  the food after draining, expressed per 100 g. The user confirms that the
  evidence describes the current recipe after those directions. A changed
  recipe, directions or nutrition evidence invalidates that confirmation.
  Separate drained weight is not required for values already per 100 g.
- Calculate a drained serving from its recorded mass. When the selected
  food state is drained and the existing serving size is in grams, reuse it
  rather than asking twice. Otherwise a separately recorded mass can be
  used; no density or volume-to-mass conversion is assumed.
- Missing nutrients, directions, evidence, serving mass and confirmation
  remain explicit review items. Known zero is distinct from an unknown
  value. The undrained recipe and drained weight do not establish nutrient
  retention or distribution.
- Show the drained basis, directions, unrounded entered values, source and
  serving calculations in review and trace. Do not present an as-sold panel
  as the drained panel. Connecting the applicable basis and columns to the
  label remains N11.
- Preserve entered details when draining is disabled. Draining combined
  with other consumer preparation or incompatible food states is not
  calculated; both paths require review instead.

## Outside this decision

Drained composition calculated from components or retention, per-100 mL
drained output, expanded nutrient rows and automatic interpretation of
directions or overlapping legal provisions remain unsupported. The
suitability of the supplied evidence for a particular product needs review.
